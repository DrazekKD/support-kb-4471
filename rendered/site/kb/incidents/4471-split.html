The three problems

parser — the shared parser reads dates month first. Every tenant on file writes the day first. A date like 7 April comes out as 4 July. Most of the reports are this one. This is the fix that is queued.

hint — booking-v3 tells people to type the month first. The booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field, so people type the month first. The form then reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. The date flips before the parser sees it. Only reports that came in through the form of a booking-v3 tenant are this one. Fixing the parser does not fix it; the form’s hint has to change.

one-day — the date moves by one day. These reports are not a flipped month. The meeting is on the day before or after, the same month. Some reporters saw it only in June, or saw two different days in two cities for the same meeting. That points at time zones, not at the date format, but these few reports are not enough to be sure. The desk already marked them as a one-day move (desk-notes.json). Before this page they were counted under parser.

Why one ticket hid three

  • Reports were merged on subject line only (decision 0001). Every problem here uses the same words: “wrong day”, “date is wrong”. Matching subjects cannot tell them apart.
  • intakeTag is a guess from the reporter’s browser, not from the tenant. It disagrees with the cause for many reports (see the table), so a tag is not proof of a cause.
  • The export pages overlap, so some reports appear twice. Each one is counted once here.
